About the Course

This course focuses on security threats to an organization’s data network such as hackers, intruders, industrial espionage and sabotage, fraud, and theft. The components of computer security architecture are studied as well as the principles of security networking protocols, encryption, fault tolerance techniques and file system protection. Additional topics covered include the protection of computer hardware and software.

Learning Outcomes

  • Identify and describe major security threats to organizational data networks, including hacking, intrusion, espionage, sabotage, fraud, and theft.
  • Apply secure networking protocols, encryption techniques, and fault tolerance methods to strengthen system defenses.
  • Analyze vulnerabilities in file systems, hardware, and software to determine potential risks.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of different protection strategies for data integrity and system resilience.
  • Design integrated security solutions that combine technical, procedural, and organizational safeguards to address real-world scenarios.

Hands-on Labs

Lab 03

Phish Desk: Triage & SETA Design

Social engineering, email authentication, SETA program design

Lab 04

Crack Bench: Hashing & Offline Password Cracking

Hash identification, dictionary and rule attacks, masks, slow hashes

Lab 05

Risk Desk: Risk Assessment & Cost-Benefit Analysis

TVA, likelihood × impact, treatment strategies, SLE / ALE / CBA

Lab 06

Access Control and Firewalls

Unix permissions, firewall simulator: ACLs, rule order, stateful filtering, DMZ

Lab 07

Cryptography Workbench

Caesar, Vigenère, XOR, hashes, AES modes, X.509, hybrid RSA + AES in CyberChef

Lab 08

Build, Break, and Defend a Blockchain

Hashes, signatures, mining, rewriting history, double spending, the 51% attack

Lab 09

Recon, Exploit, Detect

Port scanning, HTTP in DevTools, bypassing client-side controls, log analysis, detection rules

Lab 10

Outside In: Build a Secure Facility

Fences, doors and vestibules, sensors and CCTV, fire suppression, UPS and generators

Extra

Signal Watch: Wi-Fi Sees the Room

A laptop’s Wi-Fi signal strength, live. Walk past and watch it drop.

Extra

Echo Watch: Sound Sees the Room

An inaudible tone and its echo reveal movement in the room.
